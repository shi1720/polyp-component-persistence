Exploratory protocol recorded 2026-10-14, before analysis of any study predictions.
Question: Does largest-connected-component cleanup after recall calibration increase missed polyp area, and can a nested completion retain cleanup while allowing standard monotone conformal risk control?
Data: PraNet benchmark test data processed by Yeh et al. NeurIPS 2025; published fixed PraNet-19 checkpoint. No retraining. Exact decoded-pixel duplicate audit with train priority and deterministic within-test retention. No patient-level independence claim.
Primary grid: 257 thresholds equally spaced from 1 down to 0, inclusive; lambda index increases mask size. Eight-connectivity; largest component ties resolved by first raster label.
Procedures: raw threshold CRC; raw CRC then LCC; LCC recalibration (heuristic); prefix union of LCC masks, recalibrated; suffix-maximum LCC loss recalibration, evaluated on LCC output. Controls: component anchored at fixed highest-score pixel; fixed area opening (64 pixels), recalibrated. All postprocessing definitions fixed before results.
Primary target: image-averaged missed annotated area alpha=0.10. Sensitivity alpha=0.05,0.20; four-connectivity and threshold-grid resolution, where feasible.
Primary evaluation: 500 random unstratified 50/50 calibration/evaluation splits of deduplicated held-out image pool. Shared splits across methods. This is finite-pool split sensitivity, not independent replication. Mean across split risks and paired cleanup penalty; image-level bootstrap CI for full-pool fixed-threshold descriptive effect if included. Report per-source descriptive risk and area, not clinical endpoints. External source-held-out calibration is distribution-shift stress only.
Metrics: mean missed fraction, fraction of images losing >1% annotated area after cleanup, selected threshold, predicted area/image area, Dice, frequency of nonnested LCC paths, prefix-union area cost. Synthetic counterexample and exhaustive small-grid tests verify mathematical statements independently of benchmark results.
Novelty boundary: conformal risk control, morphology, component trees, running unions and monotone envelopes are established. Proposed contribution is the postprocessing audit and empirical characterization in polyp segmentation, not a new general calibration theorem.

Amendment 1, 2026-10-14 (after the first full 257-point path computation):
The uniform probability grid is too coarse for the highly saturated published PraNet logits. At alpha=0.10 every primary split selected the full-frame terminal mask. This is a numerical-resolution limitation, not evidence of a useful cleanup correction. Preserve these pilot outputs, including the null cleanup result. For the primary nondegenerate evaluation use the deterministic grid t(u)=sigmoid(10*logit(u)), u equally spaced from 1 to 0 with exact endpoints. This is equivalent (away from floating-point saturation) to uniformly thresholding temperature-10 scores, as used in the official original CRC polyp implementation. No temperature is fitted, no checkpoint changes, and alpha remains 0.10. Evaluate a 513-point version for grid sensitivity. The grid was changed after seeing the pilot's degeneracy, so this study remains exploratory.
Add a published Polyp-PVT checkpoint as a secondary architecture replication, using the same cleaned image pool and preprocessing, with the sum of its two output logits as in its official Test.py. Remove only its redundant pretrained-backbone initialization before strictly loading the complete released checkpoint. This addition precedes viewing any PVT calibration result. The original primary model remains PraNet.
